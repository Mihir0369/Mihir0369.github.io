01 · Public sector · City operations
Business question
Which 311 requests are recorded as open, and are the numbers comparable year to year?
Key finding
49.45% of 2025 parks, forestry and environment requests are recorded as open, against 1.37% to 6.60% in every other division
Business implication
Audit open-status records before reading them as a backlog.

02 · Banking · Retail lending
Business question
At what lag is credit growth most associated with earlier rate changes?
Key finding
Strongest negative association between rate changes and credit growth at an 18-month lag (correlation -0.52); observational, not causal
Business implication
Test the 18-month lag before building lending targets on it.
Skills demonstrated
Multi-source modellingLag and correlation analysisDAX time intelligenceCareful non-causal wording
Case study ↗GitHub ↗
Rate lag: pick a lag in months to compare credit growth with an earlier rate change
03 · Transportation safety · Public safety analytics
Business question
When and where do Canadian roads turn deadly?
Key finding
Among recorded fatal and injury collisions, the fatal share was 3.41% overnight against 1.52% by day (2.24x); not a per-trip risk
Business implication
Track the fatal share, not only the collision count.
Skills demonstrated
SQL validation (11 queries)Star schema with unknown membersScenario modellingDecomposition analysis
Case study ↗GitHub ↗
When: a heat map of fatal share by hour and weekday, with the night hours highlighted
04 · Healthcare · Public health
Business question
Are opioid harms falling, or only some measures of them?
Key finding
Opioid deaths fell 29.43% and EMS responses 7.84% from the 2023 peak to 2025. These are separately reported Canada totals, and each source covers different places
Business implication
Report all four measures together, with each source's coverage.
Skills demonstrated
Suppressed and missing dataCross-source comparison with stated coverageHeat and tile mapsData-quality reporting
