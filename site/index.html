Where does Toronto 311 leave requests unresolved, and can its numbers be compared year to year?

  • Five report pages plus a drill-through page, with one-click Reset filters

  • Open-request rate by division that exposes the 49.45% outlier

Banking · Retail lending

The strongest negative association between rate changes and credit growth appears at an 18-month lag

Strongest negative association between rate changes and credit growth at an 18-month lag (correlation -0.52); observational, not causal
  • Rate-lag selector (0 to 24 months) that compares credit growth with an earlier rate change
  • Policy, prime and mortgage rates side by side with household credit, 2010 to 2026
  • Year-over-year growth for total credit and each category, with a scorecard

Transportation safety · Public safety analytics

When and where do Canadian roads turn deadly?

Among recorded fatal and injury collisions, the fatal share was 3.41% overnight against 1.52% by day (2.24x); not a per-trip risk
  • Heat map of fatal share by hour and weekday, with night hours highlighted
  • 2020 against 2019, with fatal and injury collisions indexed to 2019
  • Illustrative what-if scenario (assumed effect, not an observed outcome): how much of the gap between risky and safer roads to close

Skills demonstrated: SQL validation (11 queries), star schema with unknown members, scenario modelling, decomposition analysis
